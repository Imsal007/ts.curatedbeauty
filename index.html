In their words

Caring for your results

You'll get this by email after your appointment, specific to your treatment. It's here too, so you can read it before you book.

Botox & neuromodulators

  • Stay upright and don't rub the area for 4 hours
  • No heavy gym, saunas or alcohol for 24 hours
  • Full effect settles in 10–14 days, complimentary review included

Dermal filler

  • Mild swelling or bruising for 24–48 hours is normal
  • Sleep elevated the first night, avoid pressure on the area
  • No saunas or heavy gym for 48 hours, final shape at 2 weeks

Sculptra & biostimulators

  • Follow the 5-5-5 rule: 5 minutes, 5 times a day, for 5 days
  • Hydrate well and use SPF daily
  • Collagen builds gradually over 6–12 weeks

Rejuran & skin therapy

  • No makeup or touching the area for 12 hours
  • No saunas or gym for 48 hours, tiny papules for 24–48 hours are normal
  • SPF 50 daily, courses run one session every 4 weeks

Questions people ask

Where is the clinic?

260 Pentonville Road, London N1 9JY, in Islington. It's a short walk from King's Cross St Pancras and Angel stations.

How much does Botox cost?

Three areas is £300 and four areas is £350. Single-area treatments — gummy smile, lip flip, dimple chin, DAO lift — are £200 each, and masseter is £400. Every price includes a full medical assessment.

How much is lip filler?

Lips with 1ml of hyaluronic acid dermal filler is £160. Nasolabial folds, chin and jawline are £180 each.

How long do results take to appear?

Botox reaches full effect at 10 to 14 days, and a complimentary review is included. Dermal filler settles to its final shape at 2 weeks. Sculptra builds collagen gradually over 6 to 12 weeks. Rejuran courses run one session every 4 weeks.

Do I need a consultation first?

Every treatment begins with a full medical assessment, and that's included in the price. A standalone consultation is £150, or £159 for a skin consultation, if you'd rather talk things through before committing.

Is a deposit required to book?

Yes, a 20% deposit secures your appointment and comes off the cost of the treatment.

What products do you use?

Only medical-grade products from leading medical laboratories approved by health authorities, used in authorised facilities. Nothing off-label.

Who carries out the treatment?

Tania Siguencia, a medically trained Nursing Assistant with over seven years of experience in advanced, non-surgical facial rejuvenation.
