Why clients choose Tania

With over seven years of experience, Tania's approach centres on enhancing each patient's natural beauty through personalised, safe, and considered treatment — never artificial, never overdone.

She specialises in neuromodulators, hyaluronic acid dermal fillers, collagen biostimulators, and full-face harmonisation, using minimally invasive techniques and products sourced exclusively from leading medical laboratories approved by health authorities.

Considered, natural change
